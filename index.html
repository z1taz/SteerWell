Three modes

AI assistance, on your terms.

Not every meeting is the same. Not every moment needs the same level of AI. SteerWell adapts to you.

Driver

"You think. I'll help you remember."

SteerWell gives you contextual cues, hints drawn from the actual meeting context, so you can recall information yourself. You stay in the driver's seat. The AI is a co-pilot who knows when to stay quiet.

If you need the direct answer, just ask. SteerWell will give it to you immediately.

Best for: active learners, people who want to stay engaged, and situations where your own recall matters.
Driver Mode: Active
Z What did we agree on for the API rollout?
Think back to when the backend team shared their timeline. There was a phased approach mentioned.

Passenger

"You relax. I'll handle the recall."

SteerWell directly answers your questions using the full meeting context. Answers are precise and backed by source references, the exact moment in the meeting where the information came from.

You focus on the conversation. SteerWell keeps track of the details.

Best for: back-to-back days, high-context meetings, and when you need answers fast.

Just Capture

"Record + transcribe. Nothing more."

Sometimes you just need a record of what was said. SteerWell captures and transcribes your meeting without active AI assistance. No summaries. No prompts. No interruptions.

The transcript is yours. You can switch to Driver or Passenger mode later if you want to recall something specific.

Best for: sensitive meetings, 1:1s, and situations where you prefer to process notes yourself.

Product philosophy

AI shouldn't take the wheel.

In a world where software can quietly observe, analyze, and act, SteerWell takes a different approach. It tells you when it's there, shows you what it's doing, and lets you decide how much help you want.

You take the wheel. SteerWell follows.

Use cases

Built for how you actually work.

Founders

Investor and client calls move fast. Use Passenger mode to get sourced answers instantly. Come back to Driver mode when you want to recall the detail yourself.

Team leads

Six meetings in a day means details blur fast. Passenger mode gives you the sourced answer in seconds. No rewatching. No guessing.

Creative studios
